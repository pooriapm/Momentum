## 1. Eligibility and account

- The initial service is for people aged 18 or older.
- Users must provide accurate account and eligibility information, protect their
  credentials, and notify Momentum of suspected unauthorized access.
- One person must not use another person's health profile or subscription unless
  an approved delegated-access feature and consent model exist.
- Email verification, current consent, safety screening, entitlement, and
  supported-country checks may be required before an AI feature is available.
- Account recovery, suspension, and termination procedures require final support
  and legal review before launch.

## 2. General-wellness boundary

Momentum may help organize meals, exercise, habits, and progress for general
wellness. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease; prescribe a
therapeutic diet or medication; rehabilitate an injury; or guarantee weight,
body-composition, performance, or health outcomes.

Users should consult an appropriately qualified professional before acting when
they have a medical condition, pregnancy or breastfeeding, an eating-disorder
concern, an injury, symptoms, medication interactions, clinician restrictions,
or uncertainty about safe exercise or nutrition. Momentum is not monitored as
an emergency service. Urgent symptoms or danger require local emergency or
crisis services, not the app.

## 3. AI-generated content

AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate, culturally unsuitable, or unsafe for a
particular person. Nutrition values labeled as estimates are not laboratory,
manufacturer, or professional verification. Users remain responsible for
checking ingredients, allergens, equipment, technique, serving size, and their
ability to exercise safely. Product validators reduce risk but do not make AI
output medical advice.

Momentum may refuse, delay, replace, or withdraw generated content when account,
safety, quality, quota, provider, geographic, or legal checks fail. Already-saved
safe account and privacy controls should remain available when an AI path is
disabled.

## 4. Geographic availability

Momentum serves two product versions. An Iran IP at first visit shows Persian
and rial prices. Any other IP shows English and US-dollar prices. At account
creation that version is stored as `product_region` and does not change if the
user later travels or uses a different IP. Gift, checkout, and monthly plan
generation follow the same product rules in both versions. There is no
in-product geo-block or waitlist.

## 5. Acceptable use

Users must not:

- violate law, provider policies, sanctions/export controls, or another person's
  rights;
- seek diagnosis, treatment, dangerous restriction, eating-disorder promotion,
  self-harm encouragement, unsafe exercise, or instructions to override a
  qualified professional;
- upload content they lack the right or consent to process;
- probe another account, bypass authorization/quota/safety/geographic controls,
  scrape at scale, reverse engineer protected services, distribute malware, or
  disrupt the service;
- present Momentum output as certified clinical or professional advice.

Enforcement and appeal procedures, including notice and preservation duties,
must be approved before production.
